Maya’s weekly recommendation

Protect the livestream first.

This week has three different energy lanes: urgent production, deep systems, and creative performance. The clean strategy is not to do everything every day — it is to give each day one dominant focus.

Rule for the week: livestream gets urgency, Awaken gets structure, DJ gig gets creative polish. Everything else either supports those three or goes to Parking Lot.
